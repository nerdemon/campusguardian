01 — Problem Statement

The Modern Campus is Bleeding Efficiency

Universities worldwide face four chronic, costly failures that no single system has unified: classrooms run HVAC and lighting at full power in empty rooms, faculty lose class time to manual roll-calls, security teams cannot monitor every space simultaneously, and institutional policies buried in PDFs remain permanently inaccessible. These are not edge cases — they represent billions in avoidable annual expenditure.

02 — Our Solution

A Central Nervous System for the University

CampusGuardian AI is a fully autonomous, multi-agent ecosystem that perceives, reasons, and acts across four critical campus domains simultaneously — without human intervention.

🌿

Smart Climate & Energy Engine

Real-time occupancy monitoring triggers autonomous energy-saving protocols for empty rooms — targeting up to 40% reduction in campus energy waste.

📡

Autonomous Academic Ledger

Network-layer MAC address proximity detection logs student attendance the instant their device connects to classroom Wi-Fi — tamper-proof and fully automatic.

🛡️

AI Security Evaluator

Gemini 2.5 Flash continuously evaluates every room, classifying risk as Safe, Warning, or Critical with actionable recommendations in under 5 seconds.

🧠

RAG Knowledge Agent

Campus policy PDFs are indexed into ChromaDB via n8n. Students query rules, timetables, and guidelines in natural language with cited, grounded answers.

06 — What Makes This Different

Why Team AUTOMATA's Approach Wins

Most smart campus proposals are single-feature implementations — one app for attendance, or one dashboard for energy. CampusGuardian AI is the only solution that unifies all four pillars under a single, continuously reasoning AI layer.

The Agentic Router Pattern is a key architectural innovation: standard telemetry is logged with near-zero latency while only genuine anomalies trigger LLM inference — making the system cost-efficient at scale. The result is a fully working, end-to-end autonomous system, not a prototype. Every element on the live demo — sensor feeds, MAC ledger, AI evaluations, RAG chatbot — is wired to real backend services and a live cloud database.
